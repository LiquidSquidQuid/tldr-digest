04 — How it works

From inbox to brief
in four steps.

Fully automated. No human editors. Just Claude reading, ranking, and writing.

i.

Fetch

Every morning at 5:30am, we pull the latest edition of all 12 TLDR newsletters via their RSS feeds and email archives.

fetch(rss) → parse → normalize
ii.

Deduplicate

Claude groups stories that cover the same event across multiple newsletters. Five emails about the same launch become one clean entry.

stories[] → embeddings → cluster → merge
iii.

Take

For every story, Claude writes a sharp, honest take — not a summary, but an opinion with context, critique, and connections.

story → claude.analyze() → take
iv.

Publish

The digest lands on the site by 6am. Top 5 picks are ranked, every story is searchable, and Claude's take is always visible.

digest → supabase.upsert() → live
